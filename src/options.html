InfiniTabs Settings

Keyboard shortcuts

Click a hotkey to change it. Browser-wide shortcuts open Chrome’s native editor; return here to see the actual assignment. Other shortcuts are recorded here and apply only inside the Recent switcher. Escape always cancels the switcher, even with Ctrl held.

Browser and OS shortcuts take priority. Windows may open Start for Ctrl+Escape; losing focus cancels a held switcher, but the extension cannot suppress Start. Existing installations keep their Chrome shortcut assignment until you change it.

Recent tabs

Off by default: deleted tabs are not retained by MRU. When enabled, retain up to 100 future deletions for 30 days. Restored tabs append at the current session’s end without their former groups or positions. Turning this off clears saved recovery entries, not existing logical tabs.

MRU follows the sidebar’s Live Only toggle. Configure the switcher and Previous tab shortcuts at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. Private-window MRU is not supported in this release.
